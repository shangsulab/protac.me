RESEARCH

Beyond conventional PROTACs

BioPROTACs

Genetically encoded degraders that combine target binders with E3 ligase machinery.

Peptide binders

Structure-guided discovery and validation of compact binders for difficult protein targets.

Programmable TPD

New strategies to control disease-driving proteins through engineered degradation and induced proximity.

ABOUT

PROTAC.me is an academic research portal developed by the Su Lab.

The site serves as a central home for targeted protein degradation tools, databases, and research projects developed by our academic team at Louisiana State University.

Learn more about our research at sushanglab.com.

OPEN FOR COLLABORATION

Have a protein you want to degrade—or a target that needs a binder?

We welcome academic and translational collaborations involving targeted protein degradation, BioPROTAC development, peptide-binder discovery, and related induced-proximity strategies.

Please reach out to Dr. Shang Su at ssu5@lsu.edu if you want your protein degraded or simply want to develop a binder for it.
